Intentions

What the coin is for #

The coin is meant to celebrate and support open source, and the legacy of Requests. Transparency comes first. There is no sleight of hand.

Requests began as Kenneth’s library — “HTTP for Humans” — and it now lives on in the care of the Python Software Foundation at {{ ext("https://github.com/psf/requests", "github.com/psf/requests") }}. This token is a separate thing: a memecoin on Solana. It does not change the library, and the library does not back the token.

This page does not publish a roadmap, a price target, a buyback, or a burn.

The thesis, plainly

  • A community token named after Requests, HTTP for Humans.
  • Kenneth didn’t launch it. He is its largest holder, and he took over the listing to give it an honest home.
  • The community runs on five rules.
  • Skin in the game: about 102.5M tokens (about 10.7% of supply) are locked on Streamflow, non-cancelable, until Feb 14, 2027.
  • It is not an investment product. It is a small, thinly traded memecoin with no promises of price, returns, or a roadmap. Only put in what you’re fine losing.

Creator fees from trading go to Kenneth Reitz. He uses them in two ways: buying more $requests, and supporting his growing family (for example, his wife needs a new car). This is a plain disclosure, not a promise of buybacks, price support, or returns.

The official website is requeststoken.org. Since the community takeover on Oct 24, 2026, the DexScreener listing shows only requeststoken.org and @kennethreitz42.
