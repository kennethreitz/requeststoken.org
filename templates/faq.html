FAQ

Is there an official $requests X account?

Not yet. The only official account is {{ x() }}. @RequestsToken and requeststoken.com are not his. Any new account will be announced from @kennethreitz42 and listed on this page first.

What’s the utility?

None is promised. The coin celebrates open source and the legacy of Requests. Kenneth is a web2 guy, and no web3 features are planned.

What’s next?

One day at a time. No roadmap, no promises: just the site, the code, and the community, tended day by day.

Did Kenneth create the token?

No. Kenneth didn’t launch it. He is its largest holder, and he took over the listing to give it an honest home.

Is this the Requests library, or a PSF project?

No. The Requests library and this token are different things. This token is not a Python Software Foundation project. It does not change the library, and the library does not back the token. More about the library.

Where do creator fees go?

Creator fees from trading go to Kenneth Reitz. He uses them in two ways: buying more $requests, and supporting his growing family (for example, his wife needs a new car). This is a plain disclosure, not a promise of buybacks, price support, or returns.

Is any supply locked?

Yes. About 102.5M tokens (about 10.7% of supply), Kenneth’s own holding, are locked on Streamflow, non-cancelable, until Feb 14, 2027. The lock proof links every address on Solscan.

Where do people talk?

In the group chat on X. The Telegram group is locked and read-only, and there is no Discord. See Community.

Is this financial advice?

No. Memecoins are highly speculative. There are no promises of price, returns, or value. Only spend what you can afford to lose.
