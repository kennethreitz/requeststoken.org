04 — Check the source

What the numbers can tell us

Download counts come from the public ClickPy / ClickHouse replica of PyPI logs. The daily series uses its aggregate table; the historical baseline uses a raw-table count. They can differ.

The history starts in 2016, not at Requests’ first release. Missing days are not zero. Downloads can include automated builds and repeated or partial transfers; they do not identify a company running the library.

Counts update only when a new source snapshot arrives. The countdown marks the next UTC period. No per-second estimate is added to the totals.

Download the 25 Oct 2026 audit · PyPI source documentation · Download-count definitions

Project entries are reviewed against public source code at a specific commit. SDKs, tests, examples, optional dependencies and production components keep their actual scope. Presence in a repository does not establish current company-wide deployment or endorsement of a token.

Refreshes are shared and attempted at most hourly on visits. Failed checks keep the last snapshot and wait ten minutes before retrying. Data freshness is shown separately from the time of the latest check.
